Welcome to the new NavSahyog Foundation website. We have rebuilt the site from the ground up —
faster to load on rural bandwidth, free to host on GitHub Pages, and simpler for our team to keep
up to date.

Every programme page, every annual report, and every contact detail is now in one place. If you
spot anything missing, please [write to us](/contact/) — we'd love the nudge.

Over the next few weeks we will be adding field notes from our Area Facilitators, short stories
from children in the programme, and updates from our Vanya Samriddhi food-forest pilot.
